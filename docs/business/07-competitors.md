# Competitive landscape

| | Free khata apps (Udhaar Book, CreditBook, DigiKhata, Easy Khata) | Desktop distribution software (HysabOne, Candela, local VB/Excel) | B2B marketplaces (Bazaar, Dastgyr, Retailo) | ERP (Odoo, SAP B1, local ERPs) | **Munshi** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Who it's for | shopkeepers | distributors with a PC and an operator | retailers buying stock | manufacturers | distributors on phones |
| Orders | no | yes, typed | yes (from the retailer) | yes | chat/voice/form, approval-gated |
| Godown & dispatch | no | yes | n/a | yes | yes, routes, vehicles, capacity, OTP |
| Driver on the route | no | no | their fleet | no | yes, offline |
| Khata & aging | yes | yes | no | yes | yes, FIFO, promises, templated reminders |
| Suppliers & purchases | no | yes | n/a | yes | yes |
| Reports & margin | basic | yes | no | yes | yes |
| Roles & approvals | single user | logins | n/a | complex | four roles, tool-level gates, audit with names |
| Urdu | yes | some | yes | no | yes (UI + chat) |
| Price | free (monetised via lending) | Rs 60–200k + operator | commission | Rs lakhs + consultant | Rs 4–9k/month or self-host |
| Data ownership | theirs | yours (on the PC) | theirs | yours | yours (one file, exportable, self-hostable) |

**Where Munshi loses today:** batch/expiry tracking (pharma, food), principal claim
management, GST/FBR e-invoicing, multi-currency, and an app-store listing. All are on the
roadmap; none blocks the beachhead segment.

**Where nobody else plays:** the approval-gated agent model — a business owner who can
delegate the *doing* to software and staff while keeping every *decision* — and the
customer's OTP as proof of delivery.
