# Go-to-market

## Beachhead

Agri-input distributors in South Punjab (Multan, Vehari, Bahawalpur, Khanewal, Lodhran):
a dense cluster, credit-heavy, route-based, one to three vans, owners who already run
the business on WhatsApp. The demo business is one of them. Adjacent, same motion:
FMCG sub-distributors, lubricant distributors, pharma distributors (needs batch/expiry —
v1.1), building-material suppliers.

## Motion

1. **Referral from the principal.** Fertilizer and pesticide companies have sales
   officers who visit every distributor monthly and care about dealer collections. One
   territory officer who likes Munshi introduces 30 distributors.
2. **Field demo, 20 minutes, on the owner's own phone.** Sign him up, import his khata
   from the Excel he already has (or type 20 accounts live), send *him* a reminder
   template, show the driver screen with the code. Close on the Rs 4,000 plan with the
   first month free.
3. **Onboarding call within 48 hours**: routes, vehicles, staff PINs, the owner's digest
   time. First week: the clerk uses the desk, the driver closes stops, the owner watches
   Today. Day 8: check-in call; day 30: card on file.
4. **Expansion:** every business brings its suppliers (who are distributors themselves) and
   its bigger retailers (who become the next tier of customer).

## Channels, in order of expected efficiency

| Channel | Why | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Principal sales officers (fertilizer, pesticide, seed) | trusted, visit monthly, want collections data | commission Rs 2,000/close |
| Existing AZEKTRA automation clients and their networks | warm | — |
| Local dealer associations (Anjuman-e-Tajran) | one presentation reaches 50 owners | Rs 20k/event |
| WhatsApp/Facebook groups of agri dealers | owners actually read them | content, no ads |
| Google/Facebook ads | low intent for this segment | last |

## The demo that sells

Owner's phone, his real numbers if he has an Excel, otherwise Sultan Traders:

1. *"Chaudhry Farms ko 20 urea bhej do"* → approval card → tap. "Nothing happens until you or your munshi says yes."
2. Driver mode → close a stop → wrong code refused. "The customer holds the code, not the driver."
3. Hand-in 45,000 against 50,000 → "look first at Chaudhry Farms". "It tells you where the money went."
4. *"Remind everyone over 30 days"* → templated reminders → outbox with WhatsApp links. "It never writes to your customer; you approve the template."
5. Today at 8pm on his phone.

## Objections we hear (see the sales playbook for answers)

"My munshi will resist." "My data on someone's server?" "Internet is bad on the route."
"I already use Udhaar/Digikhata." "What if the phone is stolen?" "Urdu?"

## Metrics that matter

Activation = a driver closed a stop with a real customer's code within 7 days. Retention
= the owner opened Today 4 of the last 7 days. Expansion = second business or a
supplier signed. Support load = tickets per business per month (< 1).
