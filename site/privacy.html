Privacy policy

Munshi (the app and the server software at github.com/azamali992/munshi). Last updated 9 September 2026.

What Munshi is

Munshi is business software a distributor runs for its own operation. The Android app is a shell that connects to a Munshi server chosen by the business — its own PC, its own server, or a hosting provider it contracts. Whoever operates that server controls the data.

What the app stores on your phone

  • The server address you enter, and up to four recent addresses.
  • A session token after you sign in, and your name and role, so the app opens without asking again.
  • Your language choice.
  • For drivers: deliveries closed while offline, until they are sent to the server.

Your PIN is never stored on the phone. Nothing is sent anywhere except the server you chose.

What the server stores

The business's operational records: staff (name, mobile number, role, a one-way hash of the PIN), customers and suppliers (name, phone, address, balances), products, stock, orders, deliveries, cash, expenses, messages queued to customers, and an audit log of who did what. Each business's data is a separate file that the business owner can download or delete.

Permissions

  • Internet — to reach your server.
  • Microphone — only when you tap the mic to record a voice order; audio is sent to the server, which may forward it to a speech-to-text provider (Groq) if the operator configured one. No audio is stored.

Third parties

The server operator may enable: a hosted language model (Groq) to understand chat messages — the text of the message and relevant business context is sent; WhatsApp Business (Meta) to deliver delivery codes, invoices and reminders to customers; Google Fonts for typefaces. None are required; the software works with none of them.

Your choices

Ask the business owner to correct or delete your record; owners can export and delete a business's file at any time. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on the phone.
